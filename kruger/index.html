How the “likely to see” ratings are worked out

Each animal gets a chance score for each part of Kruger, based on how many there are, where in the park they live, how visible they are by day from the road, and the season (November is summer: migrant birds are here). Bands: Very likely (80+), Likely (50–79), Good chance (25–49), Possible (10–24), Rare here (under 10). They are estimates for a few days’ visit, not guarantees.

Sources: SANParks 2023 survey trends · Lion estimate 2023–25 (Parliament reply) · Northern lion survey (EWT) · White rhino numbers · Black rhino numbers · Cheetah estimate (SANBI) · Wild dog & cheetah survey · Sabie River leopards · Northern rare antelope · Antelope by area · Regions guide
